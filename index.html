HockeyDev

Daily training plans from development to pro, with timers, a recovery check-in, and game-day fuel.

Pick a level, follow today’s session, and log how ready you feel. Plans stay on the iPhone. There is no account.

In the app

  • Levels for Development (U9–U12), U13–U15, U16 AAA, Junior / College, and Professional
  • In-season and off-season weeks, with a work and rest timer for each exercise
  • Strength, power, agility, mobility, and recovery library
  • Tap-the-light and peripheral drills, marked as extras
  • Readiness check-in and a fuel planner from body weight

A note on the plans

HockeyDev is general training education. It is not medical advice and it does not diagnose an injury. Stop if a movement hurts. Young athletes and anyone who is injured should work with a qualified coach or clinician.
