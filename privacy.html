HockeyDev Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.HockeyDev

Short version: Your level, check-ins, body weight, and best reaction time stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, and the app does not use the network.

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves a few settings on the device:

  • The level you picked and whether Off-season is on
  • Sleep, soreness, energy, mood, session effort, minutes, and the recovery checklist
  • Body weight used for the fuel amounts
  • Your best average on the tap-the-light test

Justin Novikow does not receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes this save unless it is in a device backup.

2. What the app does not do

  • No account and no sign-in
  • No ads and no tracking across other companies’ apps
  • No sale of your check-ins
  • No analytics
  • No network connection. The plans are stored inside the app.

3. Health

HockeyDev is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis. Sleep, soreness, and body weight are stored only on the device so the check-in and the fuel numbers can be shown to you.

4. Children

The development plans are written for young players and are meant to be used with a parent or coach. The app does not ask for a name, email, or any contact information.
